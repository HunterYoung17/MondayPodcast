BIG REVEAL

The CCS CUP

Between Week 10 and Week 11

Day 1: Friday(October 23rd) - Group Stages

12 Team Format: 4 groups of 3 teams (2 American groups, 2 International groups). Everyone plays each other once for a total of 2 games each (3 sets of matches total).

* Does not count towards regular season record.

Day 2: Saturday(October 24th) - Bracket Stage

Single Elimination: 4 teams (each group winner advances).
Prize: Winner of the CCS Cup gets the title and an Auto-Bid for a playoff spot!
